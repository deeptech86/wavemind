Delete your Wavemind account

You can request deletion of your Wavemind account and all associated server-side data at any time.

How to request deletion

Send an email to kijkoriakhon@gmail.com from the email address registered to your account, with the subject line "Delete my Wavemind account".

We'll confirm your request and delete your account within 7 days.

What gets deleted

Your account record is permanently removed from our server, including: your name, email, age, sex, fleet name, Fleet Knots total, latest mood state, and any crew messages you've posted.

What isn't affected

Data that only ever lived on your device — journal entries, detailed mood check-ins, breathing history, voyage details, and your profile photo — is not held by us at all, so there's nothing on our end to delete for those. Uninstalling the app removes them from your device.

See the full Privacy Policy for details on what's collected and why.
