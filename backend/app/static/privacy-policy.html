Wavemind Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Wavemind ("we", "our", "the app") is a wellness app for sailors/crew members, offering mood check-ins, journaling, guided breathing, a shared content catalog, and a crew leaderboard. This policy explains exactly what information the app collects, where it's stored, and what never leaves your device.

Who can use Wavemind

Wavemind is intended for users aged 16 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16.

Information stored on our server

When you create an account, the following is stored on our backend server and associated with your account:

FieldWhy we collect it
NameDisplayed on your crew profile and leaderboard
Email addressAccount login and identification
PasswordStored as a salted cryptographic hash only — we never store or can retrieve your actual password
AgeAccount eligibility (16+)
SexProfile field, shown only to you
Fleet name (optional)Displayed on your crew profile, if provided
Total Fleet Knots (an aggregate point total)Powers the crew leaderboard — only the running total syncs, never your individual activity log
Latest mood state (a single coarse value, e.g. "calm")Powers the anonymized "crew swell" summary other sailors see — your detailed mood answers (sleep quality, etc.) are never sent to us, only this one value
Crew messages you choose to postShown to other signed-in sailors, same as any shared message board
Session tokenKeeps you signed in; not your password

Information that stays on your device and is never sent to us

FieldNotes
Journal entriesYour private log — stored only in local, on-device storage
Detailed mood check-insSleep quality, "sea weather," homesickness answers — only a single coarse summary state (see above) ever leaves your device
Breathing/meditation session historyLocal only
Individual Fleet Knot activity logOnly the aggregate total syncs to the server; the per-activity log stays local
Voyage details (origin, destination, days remaining)Local only, never transmitted
Profile photoStored as a local file on your device only — never uploaded anywhere
Step count, sleep, and heart-rate data (if you connect Health Connect)Read live for display only — never written to any database, on-device or server, and never shared with us or anyone else

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your personal information with third parties for advertising.
  • We don't run ads or ad-tracking SDKs in Wavemind.
  • We don't access your device's health data outside of what you see live in the app — it's never stored.

Where your data is hosted

Account and crew data is stored on a server we operate, secured with encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) connections between the app and our backend at all times.

Data retention and deletion

Your account data is retained while your account is active. To request deletion of your account and associated server-side data, contact us at the email below.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the "last updated" date above.
